Bayonetta Origins · co-op mod v0.8.0

Origins Co-op Controls

Two players, one controller each. Player 1 is Cereza, Player 2 is Cheshire. Each player's whole controller drives their own character, so it doesn't matter which hand you hold things in.

Player 1 · CerezaPlayer 2 · Cheshire

Beta. Tested on the Eden emulator only; real hardware is untested and not supported yet.

The idea

The game already splits one controller in half

In the normal game you play both characters at once: the left half of the controller is Cereza, the right half is Cheshire. The mod builds that same controller from two. Player 1's controller fills in the left half, Player 2's fills in the right half.

Each player also gets the half of their controller that would otherwise belong to the other character, folded onto their own buttons. Player 1 can move Cereza with either stick, and Player 2 can attack with either trigger.

Your controller

What each button does

Filled buttons are your character's own controls. Dashed buttons are the other half of your controller, folded onto the same actions. Grey buttons do nothing.

In play

Situations

Before you start

  1. In the emulator's controller settings, set Player 1 to your first controller (or play handheld) and connect Player 2.
  2. Keep the Origins Coop mod enabled in the game's add-ons. Other code mods, such as a dynamic-FPS mod, can stay on as long as they use a different slot than subsdk8.
  3. If Player 2 isn't connected, the game plays exactly as normal, with P1 controlling both characters.

Title screen

On the title screen and the main menu, Player 1 has a normal controller: A confirms, B goes back, ZL + ZR starts, the shoulders switch tabs. Player 2 is ignored until the game starts.

Exploring together

The camera and the leash between the two are the game's own. If Cheshire wanders too far from Cereza, the game stops him at the edge, the same as in solo play.

Hug Mode

  • Either player picks Cheshire up: P1 with L, P2 with their L. It works while Cheshire is full-size.
  • While he's hugged, P1 walks. P2 aims Cheshire's stretch with their stick and stretches him out with R.
  • P2 presses R to send him back out.

Single Joy-Con, held sideways

  • Cereza: stick moves, SR is ZL (Umbran Arts), SL is L (jump, interact, hug), the four buttons work in menus and text, and the small +/− button is menus.
  • Cheshire: stick moves, SR attacks, SL is R (send out, stretch), stick click is L (hug), the four buttons are elements.

Playing solo

Disconnect Player 2 and the mod gets out of the way: one controller plays both characters, as in the original game. You can leave the mod installed.

Menus

Menus and text

The mod reads what the game is doing and switches by itself between normal play, text on screen, and menus.

Text on screen

Dialogue, Cereza's comments and voice lines while you keep playing. Co-op goes on: P2 still moves Cheshire. P1's A advances or closes the text.

⇄the game decides

Menu or pop-up

The world waits for input. P1's controller works as a normal controller: A confirms, B goes back, ZL / ZR and L / R switch tabs, sticks and D-pad navigate. P2 is ignored.

Close menus any way you like, with + or with Resume. The mod reads the game's own state, so it can't get out of step.

Known limitations

  • Map (−): still uses the co-op split instead of giving Player 1 a normal controller.
  • Controller screen: if the game opens the system controller screen (for example after a disconnect), it asks for one player and may drop Player 2. Reconnect Player 2 afterwards.
  • Button icons: with Player 1 in handheld mode and Player 2 connected, on-screen button icons may follow Player 2's controller type.
  • Tutorial windows: not every kind of pop-up has been seen in testing. If one doesn't accept Player 1's A, Player 2's A closes it. Please open an issue saying where it happened.
